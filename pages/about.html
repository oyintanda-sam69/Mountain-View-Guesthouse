Our History

A Family Heritage in Karoo Hospitality

Mountain View Guesthouse and Lodge was founded in 2011 by the Botha family, who converted a Cape Dutch-style farmhouse at the foot of Boschberg Mountain into a nine-room guesthouse. Over the past fifteen years the property has grown from a small bed-and-breakfast into a well-regarded stopover for travellers on the N10 between Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth) and Graaff-Reinet, as well as a base for hikers tackling the Boschberg trail and anglers visiting the district’s trout waters.

We remain a base for hikers tackling the Boschberg trail and anglers visiting the district’s celebrated trout waters. Every guest is greeted as a friend — every stay, personal.

Who We Are

Mission Statement

To offer warm, personal hospitality and a comfortable, authentic Karoo experience, introducing the tourist to the natural beauty and heritage of Somerset East and the Boschberg Mountains.

Vision Statement

To be recognised as the leading small guesthouse in the Blue Crane District, known for its service, sustainable practices and a deep connection to the local community.

Our Guests

Who We Welcome

  • Domestic and international leisure travellers passing through the Eastern Cape, particularly on the N10 and Blue Crane Route.
  • Hikers, birdwatchers and fly-fishing enthusiasts exploring the Boschberg area.
  • Weekend getaway couples from Gqeberha and surrounding towns.
  • Business travellers requiring overnight accommodation between the coast and the Karoo interior.
