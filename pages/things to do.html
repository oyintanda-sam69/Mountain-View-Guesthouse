Things to Do

Hiking, fly-fishing, birdwatching and the heritage of Somerset East

Hiking

The Boschberg Trail

A well-known local hiking trail right on our doorstep, with rewarding views back over Somerset East and the surrounding Karoo plains.

Fly-Fishing

Trout Waters

The district is known for its trout fishing — ask us for directions and permit information for nearby waters.

Nature

Birdwatching

The Boschberg foothills and farmland surrounding the guesthouse are a rewarding spot for local and migratory bird species.

Heritage

Somerset East Village

Explore the town's Cape Dutch and Victorian architecture, the historic Dutch Reformed Church, and small local shops and cafés.

Day Trips

Mountain Zebra National Park

A scenic drive from Somerset East, offering game viewing and further hiking within the wider Karoo region.

Relaxation

Farm & Garden Walks

Simply enjoy a slow walk around the guesthouse grounds, with mountain views and fresh Karoo air.

Plan Your Stay

Ask our team for personal recommendations and directions when you arrive — we know the area best.
