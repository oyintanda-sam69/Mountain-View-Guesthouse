Our Story

Fifteen Years of Warm Karoo Hospitality

The Botha family converted a Cape Dutch farmhouse into Mountain View Guesthouse in 2011. From a small bed-and-breakfast, we’ve grown into a well-regarded stopover for travellers, hikers and anglers exploring the Eastern Cape.

We believe in warm, personal hospitality and an authentic Karoo experience — introducing every guest to the natural beauty and heritage of Somerset East and the Boschberg Mountains.
